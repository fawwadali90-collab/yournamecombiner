Frequently asked questions

What makes a good username?
A good username is short, memorable and easy to spell. Combine your name with a keyword you love, keep it under 15 characters when possible, and avoid long strings of random numbers.
How do I get a unique username?
Fuse your name with a keyword, hobby or favorite word instead of tacking on numbers. The generator above creates blended handles that stand out, then check availability on the platform you want.
Should I use my real name in a username?
For personal brands it helps people find you, but for gaming or anonymous accounts a blended handle offers more privacy. Think about who you want to be discoverable by before deciding.
What should I do if my username is taken?
Try a blend instead of adding numbers: swap the word order, fuse your name with a keyword, or add a short prefix like the or real. The generator creates dozens of alternatives in one click.
Can I use the same username everywhere?
Using one consistent handle across platforms makes you easier to find and looks professional. Generate a favorite, then check it on each platform before committing.
