Frequently asked questions

What is a couple name?
A couple name is a playful blend of two partners' names — like Brangelina (Brad + Angelina) or Bennifer (Ben + Jennifer). It's a fun way to celebrate a relationship.
How do you combine two names for a couple?
Enter both first names in the combiner above. It tries every split point — half-blends, syllable mixes, overlap merges — and ranks the results by how natural they sound.
What are some famous couple name examples?
Brangelina (Brad Pitt + Angelina Jolie), Bennifer (Ben Affleck + Jennifer Lopez), TomKat (Tom Cruise + Katie Holmes), Kimye (Kim Kardashian + Kanye West) and Kravis (Kourtney Kardashian + Travis Barker).
Can I use our couple name on Instagram?
Absolutely — couple names make great joint-account handles. Generate a few favorites, then check our username generator for handle-ready versions.
Is the couple name combiner free?
Yes, completely free with no signup and unlimited generations.
Can I save and share our couple name?
Yes — star your favorites to save them in your browser, download the list, or copy the share link to send the exact results to your partner.
