Frequently asked questions

Is it okay to make up a baby name?
Absolutely — invented and blended names are more popular than ever. Just make sure the name is easy to spell and pronounce, and check that the initials do not spell anything awkward.
How do you combine mom and dad's names for a baby?
Enter both parents' first names above and the combiner will blend them in every possible way — front-half fusions, syllable mixes and overlap merges — so you can browse dozens of unique options.
How do I choose the right blended baby name?
Say each favorite out loud, write it down, and test it with the last name. The best blended names sound natural, are easy to spell, and feel right when you imagine calling them across a playground.
Can I filter baby name blends by gender?
Yes — use the girl or boy chips above the generate button and the results will be tuned toward names with that feel. You can also try both to compare.
Should a blended baby name work in two cultures?
If your family spans two languages or cultures, test each favorite in both. A blend that sounds lovely in one language can be tricky to pronounce in another, so try saying it the way grandparents would.
Is the baby name combiner free?
Yes, completely free with no signup and unlimited generations.
