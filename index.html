How name combining actually works

Great blends aren't random. They follow patterns your ear already loves. Our engine tries every promising split point, then keeps only the blends that sound natural.

Split at every point

Emma + Liam becomes Em+iam, Emm+am, E+liam… every split of both names is tried, in both orders.

Merge the overlaps

When names share sounds (like the "an" in Ryan and Anna), the engine fuses them at the seam for silky results.

Score the sound

Blends earn points for vowel-consonant rhythm and the 4 to 9 letter sweet spot, and lose points for awkward consonant pile-ups.

Frequently asked questions

How does the name combiner work?
Enter two or more names and the combiner blends them using methods like half-blends, syllable mixing, overlap merging and letter swaps, then scores each result for pronounceability and shows the best ones first.
Is the name combiner free?
Yes, every tool on YourNameCombiner is 100% free with no signup and no limits on how many names you generate.
Can I combine more than two names?
Yes. The flagship combiner accepts up to four names at once and blends across all of them, great for family names or group nicknames.
What is a ship name?
A ship name is a blended nickname for a couple, fandom pairing or friendship, like Brangelina (Brad + Angelina) or Bennifer. Try our ship name generator to make your own.
Can I hear how a combined name sounds?
Yes, tap the 🔊 speaker icon on any result to hear it pronounced aloud. Super helpful when choosing a baby name.
Can I save my favorite name blends?
Yes. Tap the ⭐ star on any result to save it to your favorites list, kept privately in your browser. Download the whole list as a text file anytime.
Can I share my results with someone?
Yes, every generation creates a shareable link. Send it to your partner and they'll see the exact same results.
Are my names stored or sent anywhere?
No. All combining happens entirely in your browser with JavaScript, your names never leave your device. See our privacy policy.
