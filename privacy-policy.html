Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The short version

YourNameCombiner is designed to collect as little data as possible. The names you type into our tools are processed entirely in your browser — they are never sent to our servers, never stored, and never shared with anyone.

Information we collect

  • Names you enter into tools: processed locally in your browser via JavaScript. We do not receive, store, or have access to them.
  • Favorites list: stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your device. We cannot see it.
  • Contact emails: if you email us, we receive your email address and message so we can reply. We do not share it or add it to marketing lists.
  • Basic analytics: we may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, approximate region). This data is not linked to individuals.

Cookies and advertising

We use a small number of cookies for core functionality (such as remembering your sound preference and favorites, stored locally on your device).

We may display ads served by Google AdSense or similar advertising partners. These vendors may use cookies — including the Google advertising cookie (DoubleClick) — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

Children's privacy

Our tools are family-friendly and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because tool inputs never leave the browser, there is nothing for us to collect.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have shared with us (for example via email) by contacting us through the contact page. Since tool inputs stay in your browser, you can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
