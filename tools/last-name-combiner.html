Frequently asked questions

Should we hyphenate or blend our last names?
Hyphenating keeps both names intact and recognizable, while blending creates one brand-new surname. Hyphens are more traditional; blends are shorter and feel like a fresh start. Try both styles in the combiner above.
How do blended last names work?
A blended surname fuses parts of two last names into one new name, for example, Harper + Bennett can become Harnett. Enter both surnames above to see blend and hyphenation options side by side.
Is it legal to blend last names after marriage?
Name-change rules vary widely by country and region, and some places make it easier than others. Use the combiner for inspiration, then check your local marriage-license and name-change rules before making anything official.
What last name do the kids get?
Many blended-name families give children the new shared surname, while others hyphenate or alternate. There is no single right answer, pick the option that feels fair and practical for paperwork.
Can same-sex couples blend last names?
Absolutely. Blended surnames are popular with all couples who want a shared family name without either partner giving theirs up entirely.
