Frequently asked questions

What is a brand name combiner?
A brand name combiner merges two words or founder names into a single catchy business name. It is a fast way to brainstorm startup names, product names and shop names that feel original.
How do I pick a good business name?
Aim for short, easy to spell and easy to say. Generate a long list of blends, then shortlist the ones that hint at what you do and sound confident when spoken aloud.
Should I check domain availability?
Yes — before you fall in love with a name, check whether the matching domain and social handles are available. A great name you cannot own online will only cause headaches later.
What makes a blended brand name work?
The best blends keep the strongest sound of each word, avoid awkward consonant pileups, and stay under three syllables. If strangers can spell it after hearing it once, you have a winner.
Can I trademark a blended name?
Invented words are often easier to protect than descriptive ones, but trademark rules vary by country. Treat the combiner as a brainstorming tool and consult a trademark professional before filing anything.
