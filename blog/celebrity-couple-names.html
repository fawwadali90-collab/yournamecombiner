50 Most Iconic Celebrity Couple Names Ever

Before "shipping" was internet slang, it was a headline format. For two decades, the biggest romances on Earth have been compressed into a single irresistible word, Brangelina, Bennifer, TomKat, and each one taught the world a new way to blend names.

This is the hall of fame: 50 of the most iconic celebrity couple names ever, from the Hollywood legends that invented the genre to Bollywood's finest blends and eight fictional ships too big to ignore. The Mount Rushmore: 10 names that defined the genre

These ten didn't just name couples. They taught the entire culture how couple names work.

  1. Brangelina (Brad Pitt + Angelina Jolie), the original headline machine. Their mid-2000s pairing turned the portmanteau into pop culture's default couple format.
  2. Bennifer (Ben Affleck + Jennifer Lopez), so iconic it got named twice: the early-2000s tabloid era and the 2021 reunion nobody saw coming.
  3. TomKat (Tom Cruise + Katie Holmes), four letters, zero effort to remember, instantly iconic.
  4. Kimye (Kim Kardashian + Kanye West), the alliteration made it stick; it dominated 2010s headlines.
  5. Kravis (Kourtney Kardashian + Travis Barker), the unexpected middle-slice blend, complete with matching tattoos.
  6. Haylor (Harry Styles + Taylor Swift), a brief early-2010s romance with an eternal fandom footprint.
  7. Jelena (Justin Bieber + Selena Gomez), the on-off saga an entire generation grew up following.
  8. DeepVeer (Deepika Padukone + Ranveer Singh), Bollywood royalty whose wedding broke the Indian internet.
  9. Virushka (Virat Kohli + Anushka Sharma). Cricket meets cinema in India's most-followed power-couple blend.
  10. Ziam (Zayn Malik + Liam Payne), bandmates, not a couple, proof that even friendships get ship names.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most famous celebrity couple name ever?
Brangelina (Brad Pitt + Angelina Jolie) is widely considered the most famous. It turned the celebrity name-blend into a mainstream headline format in the mid-2000s, and every Bennifer and TomKat since has followed its template.
Who were Brangelina?
Brangelina was the couple name for actors Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie, whose relationship began in the mid-2000s. The name became so ubiquitous that it essentially defined the entire genre of celebrity couple names.
What does Bennifer mean?
Bennifer is the blended name for Ben Affleck and Jennifer Lopez, "Ben" + "Jennifer" fused at the shared "n." It was coined during their early-2000s relationship and revived when they reunited in 2021.
Why do celebrity couples get blended names?
Blended names are short, memorable, and perfect for headlines and hashtags, "Brangelina" fits a magazine cover where "Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie" doesn't. Fans also use them as a term of endearment, which is why the practice spread from gossip media into fandom culture.
How can I make a couple name like the celebrities?
Use the same patterns: half-blends (Brad + Angelina → Brangelina), overlap merges (Ben + Jennifer → Bennifer), or short-stacks (Tom + Katie → TomKat). Or enter both names in our couple name combiner and pick your favorite in seconds.
